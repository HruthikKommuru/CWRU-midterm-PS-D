# Finance Analyst

## Findings

- Phone accessories: proposed price **$18/item**, stock **$4/item**, card fee **3%**. All 25 accessories respondents reported a maximum price of at least $18; this does not establish actual demand (`inputs/shopper_survey.csv`, `inputs/cost_list.md`).
- One-time setup: tables/lockbox $700, sign $150, permit $300, promotion $300 = **$1,450**. Opening inventory is **$1,200**; refundable deposit is **$2,500** (`inputs/cost_list.md`).
- Fixed operating costs: **June $8,005; July $8,181; August $8,181**. Includes 11 staffed hours/day at $16/hour, $2,500 monthly rent, and $225/month for terminal, insurance and cleaning (`inputs/mall_facts.md`, `inputs/cost_list.md`).
- Cash required before opening: **$7,650**, including setup, opening inventory, deposit and June rent. Daily model minimum cash before receipts: **$11,940.90**, within the $20,000 budget.

## Recommendation

Target **1,926 sales by August 31, 2027**: approximately **21/day**, or **2.96% of 65,037.5 projected passing visits**, assuming one item per buying visit. Traffic follows the actual calendar and includes the expected 30% August decline (`inputs/foot_traffic.csv`, `inputs/mall_facts.md`).

Break-even math: **$34,668 revenue - $25,817 setup/fixed costs - $7,800 inventory - $1,040.04 card fees = $10.96 surplus**. Buy 1,950 items in cartons of 50; assume no proceeds from the 24 leftovers or equipment. This is a required-sales target with almost no cushion, not a verified sales forecast.
