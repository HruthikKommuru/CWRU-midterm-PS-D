## Goal and audience

- June: **685 items sold at $18**, **$12,330 revenue**, approximately **2.96% of 23,122 passing visits**, assuming one item per buying visit. These are required sales targets, not verified demand.
- Offer cases, cables and grips. State the $18 price and compatibility clearly. Do not assume all 25 interested survey respondents will buy.
- Include phone owners across age groups. Test nearby students as an audience rather than assuming they dominate demand. The survey covers Saturday only.
- Focus visibility on weekend afternoons (especially 2–3 PM) and weekday evenings (6–7 PM). Weekends account for about 54.1% of typical weekly traffic. Trade all opening hours.

## Weekly review

Store staff record passing visits, buying visits, items sold, revenue, phone-model requests and how customers heard about the shop. Buying conversion = buying visits / passing visits. Items per buying visit is a separate measure. Finance compares cumulative sales with the milestones and checks cash, upcoming rent, staffing and stock purchases. Marketing reviews placement/message if cumulative sales lag target or seven-day conversion is below the model's approximately 2.96%. These review thresholds are proposed management rules.

By June 30, report actual sales, cash and weekday/weekend conversion. Update July/August expectations, including the expected 30% August traffic reduction. Use observed demand to decide whether to continue the same offer. Avoid assuming an early positive cumulative profit guarantees August break-even.
