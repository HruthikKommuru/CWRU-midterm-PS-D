# Customers and product demand

In `inputs/shopper_survey.csv`, 25 of 100 respondents (25%) selected phone accessories, compared with 60% for cheap snacks, 10% for art and craft bundles, and 5% for used books. Phone accessories are the second-most selected category. Five accessory-interested respondents appear in each of the five age groups, so the sample does not justify targeting only one age group.

Accessory respondents stated maximum prices from $18 to $25, averaging $20.60 ($515 divided by 25 responses). These are stated price limits, not actual purchases or a proven selling price. The survey groups all phone accessories together; it does not establish separate demand for cases, cables, grips, or particular phone models.

All 100 responses were collected on Saturday. The results may not represent weekday shoppers, and 25% interest must not be treated as a sales conversion rate. Nearby Northfield College has 4,800 enrolled students and summer classes, making students a potential audience, but the inputs do not show how many visit or buy accessories (`inputs/mall_facts.md`).

### Competition

No business in `inputs/competitors.csv` explicitly specializes in phone accessories. This suggests a potential opportunity within the supplied directory, but does not establish that general retailers such as Harlow's or ValueHome carry no accessories, or that customers lack alternatives outside the mall.

Snacks attracted greater survey interest but face three listed competitors: Pretzel Porch ($5.50 average purchase), Sugar Corner ($4), and Pop & Go ($3.50). The accessory recommendation therefore balances lower stated interest against fewer explicitly listed direct competitors; it is not proof that accessories will outperform snacks.

### Traffic and timing

Summing each hourly observation once in `inputs/foot_traffic.csv` gives 5,531 passing visits in the typical sample week. Saturday contributes 1,529 and Sunday 1,463, totaling 2,992 visits, or approximately 54% of weekly traffic. Both weekend days peak from 2–3 PM; Saturday records 184 visits in that hour and Sunday 178. Each weekday peaks from 6–7 PM.

These are passing visits beside Unit 42, not unique people or guaranteed customers. Prioritize weekend afternoon and weekday evening promotion, while observing the requirement to trade 10 AM–9 PM every day (`inputs/mall_facts.md`).

Harlow's closes July 31. The leasing office expects August corridor visits to be approximately 30% below the May weekly pattern, so August demand forecasts should use reduced traffic and explicitly stated purchase assumptions (`inputs/mall_facts.md`).

## Recommendation

Evaluate a phone-case and accessory pop-up offering cases, cables, and grips. Target price-conscious shoppers across age groups, with nearby college students as one potential audience. Use affordability and convenient access at Unit 42 as proposed selling points to test.

Use $18 per item as an initial pricing assumption for Finance to evaluate: it is within all 25 accessory respondents' stated maximum prices. However, those responses do not prove willingness to pay $18 for every accessory type. Confirm demand for particular phone models and product types before committing to a large assortment.

The quoted landed cost is $4 per item. Assuming a 3% card-processing fee, an $18 sale contributes $18 - $4 - $0.54 = $13.46 toward staffing, rent, and other costs (`inputs/cost_list.md`). This is not net profit or proof of break-even. Finance must test achievable sales volumes and cash requirements against the $20,000 limit.

## Handoff

- **Finance:** Evaluate the $18 price assumption, $4 unit cost, and $13.46 contribution per item assuming card payment. Calculate required sales and the implied conversion from passing visits; do not use 25% survey interest as conversion. Include the August traffic decline, $1,200 initial non-food order minimum, and carton sizes of 50 from `inputs/cost_list.md`. Check cash needs and break-even by month three.
- **Marketing:** Target a broad price-conscious audience, including nearby students. Prioritize weekend afternoons and weekday evenings. Consider free mall email listings and the central noticeboard (`inputs/mall_facts.md`). Test which products and messages generate purchases rather than assuming survey interest will convert.
- **Risk:** Address uncertain weekday demand, August traffic loss, phone-model compatibility, and unsold stock. Plan weekly replenishment and staffing for all mandatory opening hours. Verify the applicable non-food vendor permit and sign approval requirements against the inputs.
